# Milestone 4A choreography-board review

1. Open a structured project and switch between **Setup** and
   **Choreography**. Confirm both show the same project and playback position.
2. Confirm Choreography hides calibration tools and waveform peaks, retaining
   only a thin full-fit overview with outlined sections, elided names, and
   subtle marks at the configured count-group interval. Confirm wheel zoom has
   no effect. Returning to Setup restores the detailed zoomable waveform and
   toolbelt.
3. Confirm the default board uses one compact row per section, with the section
   name in that row and no separate Lead/Follow or section-title rows.
4. Drag across empty counts to create a smaller move overlay. Drag its body and
   edges; confirm crosshair, hand, and horizontal-resize cursors, whole-beat
   snapping, and Shift for fractional placement. Exercise equivalent Add,
   Rename, and Delete actions from the Edit strip and context menu.
5. Create or resize a move across a section boundary. Confirm connected
   segments select and edit one move.
6. Double-click a move label. Confirm Enter accepts it. Select the move and
   open the initially collapsed right inspector from the leftmost Edit-strip
   button. Edit label, notes, start, and duration, then collapse it again.
7. Cause a same-track overlap and assess Cancel, Trim edited move, and Shift
   later moves. Confirm Ctrl+Z reverses the entire accepted resolution and
   Ctrl+Shift+Z reapplies it.
8. Press Space outside text fields to Play/Pause. Confirm text editors retain
   normal spaces and the time label and neighboring controls do not jiggle.
9. Save, close, and reopen. Confirm tracks, moves, notes, fractional timing,
   and Structure alignment persist. Projects containing moves reopen directly
   in Choreography; projects without moves reopen in Setup.
10. Drag the red playhead in both playback views and confirm continuous seek.
    Confirm there is no redundant playback slider and the project-name field is
    right aligned in the fixed Open/Save toolbar.
11. Launch without opening a project. Confirm no demo moves are present and
    Setup is active. Change the default count group and confirm the simplified
    overview divisions follow it.
12. Drag the red playhead horizontally in both boards, then drag it vertically
    into another section row. Confirm seeking follows the pointer and may jump
    across timeline gaps. Place two moves edge-to-edge, select one, and confirm
    the shared edge resizes the selected move. Confirm the inspector toggle is
    separated from editing actions at the far right of the toolbar.
13. Scroll each board vertically to the bottom and confirm its top count ruler
    remains visible and grid-aligned. Scroll or zoom horizontally and confirm
    alignment persists. Toggle continuous ruler numbers in both boards and
    confirm the same continuous/repeating behavior and visual treatment.
14. In Choreography, use the zoom-out, zoom-in, and fit buttons, then Ctrl+wheel
    in both directions. Confirm the move board and pinned ruler scale together,
    while an unmodified mouse wheel continues to scroll vertically.
